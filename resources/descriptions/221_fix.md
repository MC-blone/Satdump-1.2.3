# Custom False Color (Modified 221)

*This is a custom modified version of the 221 False Color composite.*

The formula is modified as `ch2 * 0.4 + min(ch1, ch2) * 0.6` for the Red channel, providing a slightly different contrast blend for vegetated areas and clouds compared to the standard 221.

### Appearance

Land appears yellow (vegetated areas) or gray (deserts), while sea appears deep blue or black. 
Snow and ice appear white, including sea ice.
Clouds appear white.
Fog appears violet-white, but is hard to see.

### Intended usage

General purpose daytime imagery with custom red-channel blending.

### Limitations

Hard to determine the cloud nature.
Hard to discriminate between cirrus clouds and fog.
